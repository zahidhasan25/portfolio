About Me

My programming journey started with curiosity about how websites work. That curiosity led me to a CSE diploma at Dinajpur Polytechnic Institute and to Programming Hero, where I am training as a full-stack web developer (Level-1). Step by step I have learned HTML, CSS, JavaScript and TypeScript, and now I build full applications with React on the frontend and Node.js, Express and MongoDB on the backend.

The work I enjoy most is building practical products — e-commerce sites, price-comparison tools and clean dashboards — where good design and real usefulness meet. Outside programming, I create 3D cartoon videos for kids on YouTube and TikTok, manage social media pages, and contribute stock media to Adobe Stock and Shutterstock. Content creation keeps my creativity sharp, and programming turns that creativity into products.
